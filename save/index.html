How to send straight from TikTok
  1. Tap Install at the top (or Chrome menu → Add to Home screen).
  2. Open TikTok, tap Share on a video.
  3. Pick droptok from the share sheet. It opens with the link already filled in.

On iPhone, Safari can't put web apps in the share sheet — copy the link and use Paste here.
